Importance

DCAT is important because it makes datasets easier to find. When data is described the same way across different websites search engines and data portals can pull it together and show it in one place. This can save users time and get rid of confusion caused by inconsistent formats.

It also helps with interoperability meaning that different systems can work together without alot of extra effort. Since DCAT is machine readable computers can automatically process and organize large amounts of dataset information without manual work.

Also DCAT supports transparency. It makes it clear who published a dataset and what license it uses. This feature and history can help people trust the data and know how they are allowed to use it.
